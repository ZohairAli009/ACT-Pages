Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Introduction

This privacy policy outlines how your data is handled within the app.

Information We Collect

This app collects only the information necessary to provide its core features. This may include your daily reflections, action progress, and app preferences. All of this data is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, and we do not maintain a database containing your personal reflections or progress.

How We Use Information

Your data is used only to: • Save your daily reflections and progress. • Personalize your experience. • Improve your daily journey within the app. Your information remains on your device and is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored and protected on your own device. While we take reasonable measures to safeguard your information within the app, no device or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We recommend keeping your device protected with appropriate security measures.

In-App Purchases

This app does not use in-app purchases.

Third-Party Content

Islamic content and references are prepared using authentic sources, including Quran.com and Sunnah.com. If you visit their websites, their own privacy policies will apply.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app. Continued use of the app after updates indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or believe there is an issue with your data, please contact us through the support option provided in the app.
